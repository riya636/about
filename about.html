Origins of E-Cell

The Entrepreneurship Cell, at IIT Bombay was founded in 1998 with the motto of ‘Creating Job Creators’. Currently, we are a team of 21 managers, 2 overall Coordinators, and many enthusiastic students studying at IITB, sharing a common mission. The team meets up in an 18x18x14 room, popularly known as the ‘E-Cell office’ inside the Students Activity Centre (SAC), where the strategy to uplift the flame of startups across the globe originates.

OUR HISTORY

1998

The Entrepreneurship Cell was born as a part of a Career Management Center (CMC) for IIT Bombay in October 1998. The purpose was to promote entrepreneurship as a career option for students and assist any product or venture initiated by them.

1999

'Right Half', the first campus startup of IIT Bombay and any engineering institute in India, was founded by batchmates of the class of 2000 during their final year.

2000

Business incubator SINE inaugurated, fostering entrepreneurship and providing resources to emerging startups.
